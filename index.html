Selected project

Retail sales, end to end.

RetailPulse Analytics — Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics

End-to-end retail sales and profitability analytics project using Python, Pandas, and Power BI to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights.

  • Developed an end-to-end retail sales and profitability analytics project using Python, Pandas, and Power BI to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights.
  • Cleaned and validated 10,012 source records, removed 18 duplicate rows to obtain 9,994 records, standardized inconsistent labels, and converted key fields to appropriate data types.
  • Conducted exploratory data analysis to evaluate sales, profit, discounts, and regional performance; identified Food as the top sales category, Mumbai as the leading city by sales, and the South as the leading region by sales and profit.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard featuring sales, profit, orders, quantity, and profit-margin KPIs, with category, city, regional, and sales-trend visuals and interactive slicers for Category, Region, and Order Date.
